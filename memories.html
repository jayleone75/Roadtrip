March 30 — Day 1

The Great Departure

Every great adventure begins with someone loading the Sprinter at an unreasonable hour. Jay took the wheel and barely left it for the next eight days — the unsung hero of the trip. The kids settled in immediately, locked onto YouTube, and proceeded to watch what can only be described as the entirety of the internet. By day two, they had genuinely run out of new videos to watch. The van became its own little world at 70mph.

🚐 Sprinter life 📺 YouTube marathon ☕ Dad drove everything
🍷

March 30 — Day 1

Paso Robles & The Allegretto

The first night set the tone for the whole trip — because the Leones did not ease into things. They checked into the Allegretto Vineyard Resort, a sun-drenched Italian villa dropped into the wine country of Paso Robles. Mosaic courtyards, vineyard views, art and antiques around every corner. Dana walked in and immediately felt at home. This was her place.

First order of business: dinner at The Taste Eatery — a Guy Fieri favorite, and the family committed fully, ordering every single one of his picks off the menu. Results were… mixed. Some were hits. Some were less impressive. But that's the Guy Fieri experience — you order the whole menu and sort it out later.

Then a winery visit, where a glass of local wine somehow tasted better with vineyard views all around. The kids, bless them, kept themselves busy playing Crazy Eights in the corner so the adults could actually enjoy it. Back at the resort: the pool, the hot tub, the kind of evening that makes you forget tomorrow's a 9-hour drive day. It was perfect.

🍷 Allegretto Resort 🍽️ Guy Fieri approved 🃏 Kids played Crazy Eights 🛁 Pool & hot tub 💕 Dana's kind of place
🏜️

March 31 – April 1 — Days 2–3

Sedona & Buckeye

Red rock country. The kind of landscape that makes you understand why people move to Arizona and never leave. A quick stop to see friends in Buckeye broke up the drive south — a reminder that the best road trips weave people into the miles, not just places.

🏜️ Red rocks 👫 Friends in Buckeye

🏔️

April 2 — Day 4

The Grand Canyon

There's nothing that can prepare you for the Grand Canyon — and yet, somehow, it still manages to be exactly what you expected and nothing like it at the same time. A giant, ancient, windy hole in the earth with a river threading through the bottom. Magnificent. Terrifying. Real.

The most memorable part? The edge. The wind was whipping that day, adding a whole new dimension to the experience. Jay is happy to report that all three Leone kids have a perfectly healthy sense of mortality — they wanted absolutely nothing to do with the rim. Meanwhile, other visitors were dangling their feet over the edge for Instagram. A gust of wind away from becoming a very different kind of story.

😬 Terrifying edge 💨 Windy & cold 🎟️ Free with park pass 👨‍👧‍👦 Kids stayed back
📸 Photos coming soon
⭐

April 3–4 — Days 5–6

Zion — The Favorite

If the Grand Canyon was impressive, Zion was transcendent. The most beautiful stop of the entire trip — towering red and white canyon walls, a charming town below, great restaurants, comfortable hotels. The kind of place that makes you want to cancel the rest of the itinerary and just stay.

Which is exactly what the Leones did. After eight days of running and gunning — drive, see, go, drive — the family made a call: slow down. Cancel Capitol Reef. Stay an extra day in Zion. It was Easter weekend, spring break, and every hotel for miles was packed. Dana worked the phones. Friends were called. No luck anywhere. And then — a Best Western with a room open. Not glamorous. Absolutely perfect. An extra hike. Pool time. A real meal. A breath.

⭐ Trip favorite 🏊 Pool day 🍽️ Great food 🛎️ Easter scramble ✅ Best decision
📸 Photos coming soon
🌊

April 5–6 — Days 7–8

Pismo Beach — Dana's Place

The beach is Dana's element. Always has been. So when the family arrived at Pismo — a small, perfect California coastal town, essentially one main street worth of good things — it felt like a gift. And it was her birthday.

They added a day here too, because some places earn it. Great food. A billiards and game hall the whole family took over. And the beach — just the beach. Salt air, waves, sand between toes, the Pacific stretching west forever. The best possible way to end eight days on the road.

🎂 Dana's birthday 🌊 Pacific beach 🎱 Billiards night 🦞 Great food 🌅 Perfect ending

People at the Edge

Watching strangers dangle their feet over the Grand Canyon rim in 40mph winds. Pure terror from a safe distance.

📺

The YouTube Apocalypse

Three kids who watched so much YouTube on the road they literally ran out of new videos. The internet has limits after all.

🛎️

The Easter Weekend Hunt

Every hotel in Zion was booked. Phones were worked. Friends were called. A Best Western saved the day — and turned into a gift.

🎂

Birthday at the Beach

Dana's birthday landed on Pismo Beach. Salt air, waves, a billiards hall, and the whole family together. Perfect.

⭐

Zion Wins

Ask any Leone which stop won the trip. The answer is unanimous. Zion wasn't just beautiful — it was where the trip found its soul.

🚐

Dad at the Wheel

Jay drove the vast majority of 2,100 miles. The Sprinter van rolled on, fueled by coffee and a very patient driver.

🍷

The Allegretto

Night one at an Italian vineyard resort in Paso Robles. Dana was in heaven. The kids played Crazy Eights so the adults could drink wine in peace.

🍔

The Guy Fieri Experiment

Ordered every Guy Fieri pick at The Taste Eatery. Results were mixed. Commitment was not.
